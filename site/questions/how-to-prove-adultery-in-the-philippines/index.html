How to prove adultery in the Philippines

Adultery in the Philippines is voluntary sexual intercourse between a married woman and a man not her husband. It is a crime under the Revised Penal Code and a ground for legal separation or divorce.

What counts as legal proof of adultery

In the Philippines, adultery applies only when the married person is the woman. The law requires proof of sexual intercourse. A single act is enough if clearly shown.
