# Reply to App Review (Resolution Center), KeepMyProof 1.0

For the owner to send with the resubmission; an agent never sends it.

---

Hello,

Thank you for the review. We have made these changes in the new build:

- Screenshots and screen recording are no longer blocked anywhere in the app.
- The separate Simulator build mentioned in our earlier notes is gone. App Review now gets the same build as every customer.
- The iOS app no longer contains any review-only path: no review code, no sample or preview vault and no build switch.
- We do not hide or unlock any feature remotely: Plus comes only from an App Store purchase. Apart from checking that purchase, the one thing set from a server is which App Store prices RevenueCat offers: a one-week launch price for the lifetime purchase appears only if we make a launch offering live and the App Store returns that product. None is live, and that purchase is not part of this submission.
- The App Store text and our Privacy and Terms pages no longer refer to a preview or to purchases being "enabled"; they describe the app as submitted.

The updated App Review notes explain how to reach every feature, including Plus with a Sandbox Apple Account.

Thank you,
KeepMyProof
